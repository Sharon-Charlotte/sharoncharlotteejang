Code Queen is a women-focused digital skills and entrepreneurship initiative in Uganda designed to equip young women and girls with practical technology competencies and business exposure.
The program emphasizes hands-on training in areas such as web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), software development fundamentals, digital literacy, and innovation-driven problem solving.
Beyond technical skills, it integrates mentorship, personal development, leadership training, and entrepreneurship support to help participants transition into employment, freelancing, or startup creation.
Its broader objective is to reduce the gender gap in technology, increase women’s participation in the digital economy, and create a pipeline of confident, job-ready female tech professionals and founders.
